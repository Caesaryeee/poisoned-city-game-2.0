Now working full-time as a rideshare driver, Marcus structures his life around obligation rather than ambition. He tells himself it would only be temporary, just long enough to stabilize things.

Name: Harold

Age: 65
Ethnicity: Caucasian
Height: 5'9''(175 cm)
Hair Color: Light grey
Eye Color: Blue
Occupation: Retired manager
Dependents: Wife (his wife didn't think so)

Traits: Peaceful, Humorous, Amicable, Forgetful, Offhand

Main Archetype: Sage
Sub Archetype: Wise Old Man

Exposition: Harold, now retired, spent most of his later career leading a small, largely overlooked company. In his youth, he had been ambitious and capable, but at fifty he was unexpectedly laid off. An eccentric billionaire took a quiet interest in him and provided just enough funding to start a modest subsidiary under a larger family enterprise. Harold’s initial plan was uncomplicated: hire a handful of people, use the budget responsibly, and let things run their course. What followed was less strategic than steady. He never claimed to be a visionary businessman, and spreadsheets rarely held his attention for long. He delegated easily, trusted perhaps a little too freely, and approached problems with a shrug and a joke before anything else. Yet his calm presence diffused tension, and his gentle humor made setbacks feel survivable. Under his relaxed guidance, the company never grew spectacularly—but it endured.
In retirement, Harold lives much as he managed: unhurried and content. He fishes, hikes, and drifts easily through his days. Former employees still reach out from time to time, knowing he will answer warmly—though he may forget small details or wander off-topic before offering advice. What he gives is rarely precise instruction, but perspective, reassurance, and the sense that things will likely be fine.
